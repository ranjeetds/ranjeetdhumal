Jeff Clune, “AI-GAs: AI-generating algorithms, an alternate paradigm for producing general artificial intelligence,” arXiv:1905.10985, 2019.

This is a position paper. It reports no new experiment. Clune names the dominant approach the manual one: people design the pieces of an intelligent system and hope to assemble them. He proposes the alternative of an AI-generating algorithm, which would learn how to produce general intelligence rather than have that procedure written down. He says three lines of work are required. Meta-learn the architectures. Meta-learn the learning algorithms. Generate the learning environments, which he argues is the least developed of the three and the hardest. He also argues that this path has safety and ethical problems the manual path does not, and that the field was under-investing in it.

The paper does not show that such an algorithm exists, and it does not show that a learned generator would keep the test of “general” fixed while it learned. It is a map of the research, with a recommendation about where to spend effort.
