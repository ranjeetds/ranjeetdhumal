WO2019097412A1 was filed by Asquared IoT Pvt Ltd on 14 November 2018 and published on 23 May 2019. It claims priority from Indian application 201721040616, filed on 14 November 2017. The inventors, in the order given on the publication, are Sanjay Singh, Ranjeet Dhumal, Aniruddha Pant, and Anand Deshpande.

The application describes a device placed near a piece of equipment. A microphone collects sound, including sound outside the range a person hears. A camera collects images or video. A processor runs analytics models on both — the publication names sound-classification models, acoustic-feature models, neural networks, and deep-learning models — and identifies performance by combining the two. An analytical subsystem can update those models from what the device has just recorded, and can add a model obtained from somewhere else. The examples in the description include a manufacturing machine, a leak in a compressed-air or gas line, and the health of a vehicle. On a detected fault the processor can notify a phone or a computer.

Google Patents lists the status as ceased. The European Patent Office was told the application designated Europe, and the application did not enter the European national phase.
