status: shipped
year: 2026
stack: [TypeScript, Next.js, ElevenLabs, Claude]
problem: >
  High-stakes spoken practice — an interview, a negotiation, a disagreement —
  usually happens alone, or with a person who will not interrupt the weak point.
  Written feedback arrives after the moment in which the answer should have changed.
approach: >
  A practice session is a spoken loop. The person picks a scenario and a voice,
  hears the question, answers into the browser, and receives a follow-up aimed at
  the weakest part of what was just said. Scoring is a separate call, across
  clarity, structure, specificity, impact, and confidence, with the preferred
  answer read back aloud. Company-specific interview material and debate evidence
  are fetched at session time rather than written into the prompt.
outcome: >
  A shipped hackathon project with a public repository and a live site. What it
  demonstrates is the split between the voice loop and the scoring pass, and a
  session that can be grounded in a page fetched for that conversation. It does
  not report a measured improvement in anyone's speaking.
