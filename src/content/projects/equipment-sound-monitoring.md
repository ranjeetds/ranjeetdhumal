  A machine can announce a fault in its sound before a shutdown. Wiring a

  Models that estimate overall equipment effectiveness from industrial sound,
  with streaming and synchronization automated on AWS. Built at Asquared IoT
  from February 2017 to July 2018. The monitoring work was published as

  A monitor that listens, rather than mounting a sensor for every reading.

  as ceased.
