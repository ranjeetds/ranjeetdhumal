Lessons are MDX beside this file, in a folder named after the domain.
The filename is the visualization id, which is the URL under `/studio/`.
Files and folders starting with `_` are not published.

Chapters use the Chapter component. Equations use Equation with a TeX string.
A check uses Check, and its answer must be one of its choices verbatim.
Do not describe a control the figure does not have. Do not cite a paper that is not real.
A planned module is named in `src/data/studio.ts` and does not get a page.
